Monday 23 November 2026 on one page: 48 stories from 7 regions and 7 fields, all first reported that day (UTC). Three leads (a German-media report that Russia may put a nuclear weapon in orbit, the Asian stock crash as the AI sell-off spread, Iran vowing to break the US blockade of Hormuz), twelve illustrated cards from Benghazi to the Southern Ocean, a map of where each story happened, 33 one-line briefs and a check box naming the 22 November stories left out. Stories were gathered from 26 news feeds and cross-checked with a Perplexity Computer research pass on Gemini 4 Neon; the 15 flat pictograms were drawn by Neon too, and the page was laid out with Pillow.

`main.py` hands the committed `entry.png` (1024×1024 PNG) to the grader.

## Run it

```sh
python main.py   # writes entry.png to $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR, or /output in the make.fun sandbox
```
